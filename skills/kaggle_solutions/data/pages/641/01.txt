After completing all this work, I used 15 articles for repetition (later reduced to 12, as I deleted 3 articles due to concerns about impurity，and it's conor's great advice). The seed I used was 1144. This might be a bit abstract, but I didn't exchange many times like Conor's team did, because the seed Jagat provided at the time was 1143. I simply added a 1 and tried it out, which turned out to be quite lucky and gave me more time to deal with the purity of the articles. (Actually, I think Jagat might have figured this out earlier, but his articles included scores of 9, 9, 9).

In the final few days, I stopped submitting because I felt that the test results had an element of luck to them, so there was no need to push myself too hard. We should enjoy the process of the competition.

Lastly, my thanks and respect go to Conor(@conormacamhlaoibh)and his team. Everyone who has been in this competition from start to finish knows that he is the true champion.

10,000 attacks" is an exaggeration, but I did find more than three methods of attack. The attacks using Korean and inserting many instances of the number nine are ones I experimented with. I found that using Korean can consistently yield a score of 0 from Qwen, but Phi also gives some zeros. In my previous tests, inserting many instances of the word "nine" into articles could make Phi give a score of 9, as Phi tends to assign very low scores to nonsensical strings of words. Therefore, I have been trying to figure out how to get it to consistently give a score of 9. For words in Qwen's vocabulary, you only need to extract the English confidence scores and place them into a word list. I found that both Gemma and Llama assign a score of 0 to such articles, which is why I never believed the models in question were Gemma or Llama. In fact, during the discussion about the judging criteria, I asked if there could be models released after the competition started, such as Phi4, because I had confirmed at the time that Mistral was not being used. Later on, I ended up using Phi3.5 for my tests.
