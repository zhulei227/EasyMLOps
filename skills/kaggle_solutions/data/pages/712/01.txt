Solution Writeup · 1st place · Jul 16, 2026

1st PLACE SOLUTION

Thank you everyone for a great competition. We will publish our full solution in the upcoming days!

UPDATE:

We published our pipeline overview here.
We published techniques for prompting here
And more solution information will come soon! Stay tuned!
TEAM KAGGLE AGENT

It was a pleasure working with @titericz @jiweiliu @maxjeblick @arc144 and ChatGPT, Codex and Claude Code!

DASHBOARD

We used our dashboard to track our progress and share our onnx with each other. Visit the dashboard here to see how we progressed and what score we achieved for each task!

SAMPLE AGENT

An agent's success depended on all the information it had access to and the agent's prompt/instructions.

ARCHITECTURAL REWRITES

From the history of each task's score, we observe that architectural rewrites averaged +0.5 boost per task whereas optimzing existing graphs averaged only +0.05 per task. Therefore agents were always encouraged to explore and find better architectures. This avoided being stuck in score plateaus.

STAY TUNED

We will publish our full solution in the upcoming days!

Congrats — and thank you for putting numbers on the rewrite-vs-polish question; +0.5 vs +0.05 per task is the number people will be quoting from this competition. One way to see how lopsided that is: score is linear in ln(cost), so +0.5 points erases ~39% of a task's whole bill while +0.05 trims ~5% — one architectural rewrite is worth ten polish passes. I read that as the metric's whole message: a rewrite changes which tensors exist at all; polishing only shrinks the ones you kept.
