Sorghum -100 Cultivar Identification - FGVC 9

Solution Writeup · 1st place

Thanks to Kaggle and the hosting team for an interesting competition.

summary

My method is very similar to that of the third place(sorghum-id-fgvc-9/discussion/328593).

The main differences are as follows:

I used convnext base network as backbone.
I trained 5-folds models and ensembled them.
I combined this contest data with fgvc8data to generate a new dataset with 223 categories instead of 100.
results
step	method	Public Score	Private Score
1	convb+100categories	0.953	0.952
2	convb+223categories	0.955	0.954
3	ensemble(step1+step2)	0.959	0.957
4	step3+pseudo label	0.963	0.962
5	step4+5folds	0.965	0.965
