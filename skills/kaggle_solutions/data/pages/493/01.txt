Herbarium 2022 - FGVC9

Solution Writeup · 1st place

Optimization on single model:

change	public	private
swin-B 224 baseline	0.77223	0.78442
add cross-entropy losses on genera,species, family	0.7829	0.79544
learning rate from 2e-4 to 5e-4	0.79444	0.80501
+5crop (resize 256, 288, 320, 384, 448 and center crop 224)	0.80088	0.80981
replacing cross-entropy by subcenter-arcface with dynamic margins	0.81532	0.82267
+ extra fc by cross-entropy, add to subcenter-arcface output after softmax	0.82201	0.82929
+ data augmentation the same with Swin-Transformer	0.8315	0.83554
from swin-B224 to SwinB384	0.8468	0.85245

Fusion： we utilize the scores on public dataset as the weight.

fusion approach	public	private
fusion models (0,1,2)	0.86366	0.8679
fusion models (0,1,2,3,4)	0.86619	0.87185
fusion models (0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7)	0.87193	0.87662

Other settings: Batch size is always 512 (256 for big models), trained with 100epochs. torch.amp is adopted. Following solutions， we adopt similar Post-Process and obtain 0.001 improvement. We use Swin-B pretrained on Imagenet22k for initialization, for other backbones, we try to use Imagenet22k pretrained weights if possible. We freeze partial layers to enlarge the batchsize during the training process, We then reduce the batch size and train the full parameters.

Something less effective: class-aware sampling data cleaning
